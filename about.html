About us

Northside Robotics is a student-run team of builders, coders, and designers. We compete in robotics tournaments, but what we care about most is learning how to solve hard problems together.

Our mission

We give students hands-on experience in engineering, programming, and teamwork, no matter their background. Every member works on the robot, and we teach each other as we go.

What we value

Learning first

Mistakes are part of the process. We review what broke and why, then fix it.

Teamwork

Every sub-team depends on the others. Good communication wins as many matches as good design.

Gracious professionalism

We compete hard, share ideas freely, and help other teams succeed.

Our story

  1. 2020

    Founded

    A small group of students and one teacher started meeting after school in the shop.

  2. 2022

    First competition

    We entered our first tournament as a rookie team and built our first full robot.

  3. Today

    Growing every year

    We now have 32 members, regular outreach events, and a team that keeps getting stronger.

Where we meet

We build in Room 114 at Northside High School on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 3:30 to 6:00 pm, with extra build days during competition season. Come check us out.
